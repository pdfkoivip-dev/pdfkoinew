## Shared Design Tokens (apply to every pin — do not repeat per pin)

These are the constants. Each pin below only adds its unique hook + visual concept.

- Canvas: 1000 × 1500 px, 2:3 vertical, PNG/JPG.
- Brand: primary blue #0A84FF, accent violet #A855F7, off-white background #f8fafc, dark-navy type #0f172a, success green #10b981.
- Layout (Z-pattern): eye enters top-left hook → sweeps diagonally through the center proof visual → exits on the bottom CTA.
- Top block — Hook: large bold sans-serif, sentence case (not ALL CAPS, not Title Case), dark navy. One idea. Reads at thumbnail size (~156 px wide).
- Center block — Proof visual: show the before → after or the concrete outcome. Use a left-to-right violet arrow with a small caption. Numbers beat adjectives.
- Trust strip (quiet, small): Free  ·  No signup  ·  Runs in your browser — pick the 2 that fit; never more than 3.
- Bottom CTA pill (violet #A855F7 fill, white label, ends with →): action + "free".
- Footer: pdfkoi.com in low-weight type, bottom-right.
- Voice rules (US market): lead with the reader's problem, not the brand; one idea per pin; no emoji-as-bullets; no "Say goodbye to / Unlock / take it to the next level"; avoid an em-dash in the description body (fine in the field labels here).
- Privacy claim: PDFKoi converts client-side in the browser — "your file never leaves your device" is accurate (confirmed in tool copy: "All processing happens in your browser"). Keep this as the recurring differentiator vs. tools that upload.

Limits enforced everywhere: Title ≤ 100 chars · Description ≤ 500 chars (Pinterest hard cap; the brief said 650 but 500 is the real ceiling).

# Publishing playbook (quick)

- Spacing: 3–5 pins per tool, spread across 4–7 days. Never dump a tool at once.
- Cross-board reuse: the same conversion can post to 2–3 boards; only change the hook line and the board. (e.g., heic-to-pdf → Free PDF Tools + Students + Remote Work.)
- Rich Pins: add alt text per image describing the before→after for accessibility + SEO.
- CTR levers (highest to lowest): before/after visual → concrete number → privacy ("no upload") → free/no-signup. Put at least one in every hook.
- A/B next: for the top 5 tools, run two pin images (pain-hook vs. result-hook) and keep the winner based on outbound clicks in Pinterest Analytics.
- Accuracy guardrail (your board 06 rule): every privacy claim here is true because conversion is client-side; if you ever add a tool that does upload, drop the "never leaves your device" line from its pins.
- Titles: sentence-case the hook text on the image; keep Title Case only in the pin Title field. All titles above are ≤ 100 chars; all descriptions ≤ 500.
